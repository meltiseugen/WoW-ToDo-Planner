# Legion: locatii si ghiduri de colectare

168 iteme disponibile au destinatii Map si ghiduri: 10 pets, 9 toys, 149 cosmetics. Un wrapper retras ramane in date si este ascuns din UI. Achievements nu fac parte din aceasta modificare.

Pentru retetele cu questuri, catalogul urmareste prerequisitele neterminate ale personajului. Coordonatele de mai jos sunt sursele finale inregistrate; destinatia din joc poate fi un pas anterior al lantului.

### Enchanted Cauldron (1699)

Learn Legion Enchanting and follow Enchanter Nalthanis's profession quests in Broken Isles Dalaran (38.3, 40.3) until An Enchanting Home. Buy Formula: Enchanted Cauldron for 50 Arkhana, learn it and craft the pet. Alternatively, buy the crafted pet or a caged copy from another player or the Auction House.

- Stormheim: 39.4, 42.6 (`/way #634 39.4 42.6`)

### Ridgeback Piglet (1756)

Learn Legion Alchemy from Deucus Valdera in Broken Isles Dalaran (41.3, 33.4). Complete the alchemy-station quest chain through Return the Filter, then buy Recipe: Wild Transmutation from Patricia Egan in the same shop (42.2, 32.3). Perform Wild Transmutation at the Dalaran Alchemy Station each day until you discover Transmute: Meat to Pet. Use that recipe to make a Pulsating Sac and wait 7 real-time days for it to become a Growling Sac. Open it for a random pet; Ridgeback Piglet is one possible result. Repeat if needed, or buy a caged Ridgeback Piglet on the Auction House.

- Dalaran (Broken Isles): 42.2, 32.3 (`/way #627 42.2 32.3`)

Surse: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213256/transmute-meat-to-pet), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/item=137599/pulsating-sac), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=39338/return-the-filter), [Date ATT](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f8113a527789bf361e070d7cde94d1f57a22b2b8/db/Standard/Categories/Professions.lua)

### Knockoff Blingtron (1806)

Accept Blingtron 6000's daily gift quest and open the Blingtron 6000 Gift Package for a chance at Knockoff Blingtron. Another player's Blingtron works; it is portable and has no permanent spawn. To summon your own, progress Legion Engineering with Didi the Wrench in Broken Isles Dalaran (38.6, 25.0), complete Short Circuit and Oil Rags to Riches, craft Reaves Module: Bling Mode and install it on Reaves. Summon Reaves with a battery and choose Bling Mode. The marker follows this engineering unlock route, not a fixed Blingtron spawn. Alternatively, buy a caged pet on the Auction House.

- Dalaran (Broken Isles): 38.6, 25.0 (`/way #627 38.6 25.0`)

Surse: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/item=132892/blingtron-6000-gift-package), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=40881/oil-rags-to-riches), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/legion-engineering), [Date ATT](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f8113a527789bf361e070d7cde94d1f57a22b2b8/db/Standard/Categories/Professions.lua)

### Fel Lasher (2117)

Learn Legion Herbalism, unlock Argus through The Hand of Fate and follow the Argus campaign to open its zones. Gather herbs in Krokuun, Antoran Wastes or Eredath: Fel-Encrusted Herb nodes and Astral Glory can yield Fel Lasher. Keep gathering; this is a rare bonus from harvesting herbs, not a wild pet you trap or a stationary creature drop. The map opens the actual gathering zones because herb nodes move and respawn. You can also buy a caged Fel Lasher from another player or the Auction House.

- Zone de farm: Krokuun, Antoran Wastes, Eredath

Surse: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/npc=128137/fel-lasher), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/legion-herbalism), [Date ATT](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f8113a527789bf361e070d7cde94d1f57a22b2b8/db/Standard/Categories/Craftables.lua), [Date ATT](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f8113a527789bf361e070d7cde94d1f57a22b2b8/db/Standard/Categories/Professions.lua)

### Fossorial Bile Larva (2121)

Learn Legion Skinning and unlock Argus through The Hand of Fate. Kill and skin beasts on Argus for a rare chance at Fossorial Bile Larva; the pet comes from skinning, not their normal loot or pet battles. One reported farm is Chitinbuk Ruinwalkers in Eredath around 52.0, 35.0. Panthara in Krokuun's Petrified Forest and other skinnable Argus beasts are alternatives. Keep skinning until the pet drops, or buy a caged copy on the Auction House. The marker is a farming area, not a guaranteed pet spawn; Fiendish Leather ranks improve material yield but a particular rank is not established as a pet requirement.

- Eredath: 52.0, 35.0 (`/way #882 52.0 35.0`)

Surse: [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/item=153057/fossorial-bile-larva), [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/legion-skinning), [Date ATT](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f8113a527789bf361e070d7cde94d1f57a22b2b8/db/Standard/Categories/Craftables.lua), [Date ATT](https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings/blob/f8113a527789bf361e070d7cde94d1f57a22b2b8/db/Standard/Categories/Professions.lua)
