


WoW-specific differentiators
- Character availability: level, faction, class, profession, and expansion eligibility.
- Attempt tracker: “Completed this week on 4 of 7 eligible characters.”
- Collection task progress: automatically finish mount, pet, toy, or achievement tasks when collected.
- Route tasks: convert selected map pins into an ordered checklist.
- Location awareness: surface relevant tasks when entering a map or zone.
- Group readiness: mark tasks as solo, dungeon, raid, PvP, or group-finder compatible.
- Currency goals: current amount, required amount, weekly cap, and estimated remaining runs.
- Reset briefing on login: a compact summary of what expires soon.
- Completion history: show weekly consistency and unsuccessful collection attempts without turning the addon into a heavy analytics dashboard.


Other ideas: 

Highest-value features
1. Reset-aware recurring tasks
   - Daily, weekly, seasonal, and custom recurrence.
   - Display “Resets in 2d 4h” directly on cards.
   - Store each completion occurrence instead of simply changing the same card back to To Do.
   - Rollover choices: reset automatically, carry unfinished work forward, or skip the missed occurrence.
   - Region-aware reset schedules.
Recurring cards and scheduled automation are central productivity-board features; adapting them to WoW resets would give the Planner a real identity. Trello recurring cards, Trello automation.
2. “Today” and “This Reset” smart views
These should be generated views, not additional boards:
- Due before daily reset
- Due before weekly reset
- In Progress across every character
- High-priority unfinished tasks
- Tasks available in the player’s current zone
- Overdue or carried-forward tasks
Each card should retain its board badge so users know which character owns it.
3. Checklists and measurable progress
Tasks could contain steps such as:
- Kill four weekly bosses
- Collect 3/10 currency items
- Visit six rare locations
- Complete achievement criteria
- Buy required vendor items
Cards would show 3/6, a small progress bar, and optionally auto-complete when every step is finished.
4. Task templates and bundles
Useful built-in templates:
- Weekly raid clear
- Reputation routine
- Rare rotation
- Currency farm
- Profession cooldowns
- Mount attempt across multiple characters
- Achievement meta checklist
A bundle could create one Global parent task with character-specific attempts underneath it.
5. Session planning
Add an “I have…” planning mode:
- 15 minutes
- 30 minutes
- One hour
- Solo
- Group content
- Current zone only
Tasks would gain optional estimated duration, group size, and location metadata. The Planner could assemble a short session queue from eligible tasks.
